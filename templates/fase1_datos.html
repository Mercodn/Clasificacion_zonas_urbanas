Business & Data Understanding

Define the problem, review the dataset, and document key assumptions for the light pollution classifier.

Business objectives

  • Understand the distribution of nighttime illumination in Cundinamarca.
  • Build a classifier that assigns points to six pollution zones.
  • Provide actionable insights for environmental monitoring.

Data understanding

  • Satellite radiance values from VIIRS / SUOMI-NPP.
  • Geographic coordinates cover urban and peri-urban zones.
  • Derived features support model training and interpretation.
