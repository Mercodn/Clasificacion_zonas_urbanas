CRISP-ML Methodology

A structured approach for machine learning projects with business, data and engineering phases.

What is CRISP-ML?

CRISP-ML extends traditional CRISP-DM for machine learning by emphasizing data preparation, governance, and deployment.

  • Business understanding
  • Data understanding
  • Data engineering
  • Modeling
  • Evaluation & deployment
